Here's the order to do things in. Each step depends on the one before it, so finish one before moving on.

## Week 1: Get the app working perfectly
**Step 1: Fix the build (30 min)**
- On GitHub, the top level of the repo must show: `public/`, `worker.js`, `wrangler.jsonc`, `README.md`.
- If they're inside an extra folder, go to Cloudflare → **Settings → Build → Root directory** and type that folder name.
- Put your **KV ID** into `wrangler.jsonc`.
- Check that the **`VAPID_PRIVATE_JWK`** secret is added in Cloudflare.
- Click **Retry build** and wait for a green check. Send me the red error lines if it fails.

**Step 2: Test on your phone (30 min)**
- Open safarsalahapp.com. The bottom should show the latest version.
- Add it to the Home Screen and turn on **Prayer notifications**. You should receive "Notifications are on".
- Close the app and lock the phone. Check that the next prayer notification arrives.
- Check today's times against your masjid.
- Check that the compass beeps at the Qibla.

**Step 3: Finish the small setups (30 min)**
- **Feedback form:** send yourself a test and click the FormSubmit confirmation email.
- **Ko-fi:** connect **PayPal + Stripe**.
