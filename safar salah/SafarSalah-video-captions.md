**YouTube Shorts title**
Travelling? How to combine prayers on a long flight (jama' & qasr) #shorts

**Caption**
On a long trip you can shorten and combine prayers. Here's one plan for a 14-hour Dallas → Doha flight:
1️⃣ Dhuhr + Asr at the airport before boarding
2️⃣ Maghrib + Isha in the air
3️⃣ Fajr in the air (can't be combined, only a 1-hour window)
4️⃣ Dhuhr + Asr in the air
5️⃣ Maghrib + Isha after landing

This follows the Shafi'i view. Rulings differ, so ask your local scholar.
